# D2C Brands - Funding Research Report ($500k - $5M, 2023-2025)

**Research Date:** November 9, 2025
**Funding Range:** $500,000 - $5,000,000
**Timeframe:** Last 2 years (2023-2025)

---

## Executive Summary

This report identifies D2C (Direct-to-Consumer) brands that have raised funding between $500k and $5M in the last 2 years, along with their publicly available contact information. The research covers multiple categories including beauty, food & beverage, consumer goods, and more.

**Key Findings:**
- Total brands identified: 15+
- Geographic distribution: India (majority), US, UK, Germany, Kenya
- Top categories: Beauty & Personal Care, Food & Beverage, Consumer Goods
- India ranked 2nd globally in D2C funding ($757M in 2024)
- US led with $2.53B in 2024 funding

## MARKET INSIGHTS

### Global D2C Funding Trends (2023-2025)

**2024 Global Funding:**
- Total: $3.9B (18% decline from 2023's $5.3B)
- US: $2.53B (global leader)
- India: $757M (2nd globally, 18% decline from 2023's $930M)
- Southeast Asia: $32.5M (208% increase from 2023)

**Stage-wise Distribution (India 2024):**
- Early-stage: $355M (25% increase)
- Seed-stage: $141M (18% increase)
- Late-stage: $261M (50% decline)

**Top Funded Segments (India 2024):**
